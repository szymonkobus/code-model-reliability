Definitions
  • Task difficulty — a task's position on one scale defined from the population of models: the average model's failure rate on the task, on the logit scale; positions print as a share of the scale from 0 to 100%.
  • Failure rate — a model's share of failed attempts on a task; the task dots show it as observed, (failures + ½) / (attempts + 1), kept inside the frame.
  • Failure curve — a model's fitted failure rate against task difficulty, a Bayesian fit to its results on every task (the attempts that fail out of the attempts made); one curve per model, a thinking variant its own model.
  • Average rate / Median task — two summaries of a fitted curve: the average failure rate over the tasks at each difficulty, or the median task's failure rate; the bands and the crossings follow the definition chosen.
  • Median failure rate / Median difficulty — what the drawn curve is the median of: at each difficulty, the median over the fit's draws of the failure rate; or at each failure rate, the median over the draws of the difficulty at which the draw crosses it. The second passes through the printed crossings.
  • Uncertainty band — the central 80% or 90% of the fitted curve's draws at each difficulty; under Median difficulty, of the crossing's draws at each failure rate, drawn sideways.
  • D50, capability — the difficulty at which the model's fitted failure rate crosses one half.
  • D99, reliability — the difficulty at which it crosses one in a hundred.
  • Pin — the curves slid sideways so that each crosses the pinned failure rate at the frame's centre; the horizontal axis then reads difficulty relative to each model's own crossing, and the shapes compare directly.
  • Partial model — a model with attempts on fewer than 90% of the tasks; hidden by default and drawn faded by the Partial models switch.
  • Training run — a model trained in this project, read at several steps; its positions sit in their own row and open deselected; checkpoint 0 is the model the run started from.
