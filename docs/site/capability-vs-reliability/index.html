What the fitted line means

Definitions
  • Task difficulty — a task's position on one scale defined from the population of models: the average model's failure rate on the task, on the logit scale; positions print as a share of the scale from 0 to 100%.
  • Failure curve — a model's fitted failure rate against task difficulty, a Bayesian fit to its results on every task (the attempts that fail out of the attempts made).
  • D50, capability — the difficulty at which the model's fitted average failure rate crosses one half.
  • D99, reliability — the difficulty at which it crosses one in a hundred.
  • Average rate / Median task — two summaries of a fitted curve: the average failure rate over the tasks at each difficulty, or the median task's failure rate; the crossings follow the definition chosen.
  • Uncertainty band — the central 80% of the fitted crossing's draws; a readout prints it in square brackets after the number.
  • Bound — a crossing that lies below the easiest task or beyond the hardest, drawn at the scale's end as an open chevron pointing to the side it lies beyond, and kept out of the fitted line.
  • Partial model — a model with attempts on fewer than 90% of the tasks; hidden by default and shown greyed by the Partial models switch, never in the fitted line.
  • Training run — a model trained in this project, read at several steps; its positions sit in their own row and open deselected; checkpoint 0 is the model the run started from.
